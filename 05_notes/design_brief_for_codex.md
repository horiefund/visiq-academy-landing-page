# VISIQ Academy Design Brief for Codex

## Goal
Create a trustworthy, elegant website for elementary school parents that still feels warm and child-friendly.

## Preferred Visual Direction
- Base: 上品・信頼感重視タイプ
- Add a small amount of the warmth from the kids-friendly version
- Use the official VISIQ Academy logo exactly as provided
- White background, dark navy typography, subtle rainbow accent inspired by the logo
- Do not make it look childish or overly cute

## Visual Keywords
- clean
- elegant
- educational trust
- future-facing but not cold
- light illustrations of children are okay

## Do Not
- Do not use noisy gradients as the main background
- Do not overuse rainbow colors
- Do not present ranks as hierarchy or competition
- Do not hardcode pricing, schedules, campaign text, or changing application details

## Recommended Website Structure
- Home / Top
- VISIQ Academyとは
- 学びの流れ
- VISIQ検定・ランク制度
- 無料体験会
- お問い合わせ

## CTA Direction
Primary CTA: 無料体験会のご案内を見る
Secondary CTA: お問い合わせ
